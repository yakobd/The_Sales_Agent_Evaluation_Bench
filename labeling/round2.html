Tenacious-Bench labeling: round 2

Label each email on the 5 dimensions using only the brief and thread shown. Don't look at the repo, the evaluator or round 1 while labeling. Progress is saved in this browser. When finished, click Download CSV and move round2_labels.csv into the labeling/ folder.

Rubric
  • grounding: PASS if the email uses at least one SPECIFIC signal from the brief or thread (a funding round, the layoff, the new leader, a named role count, the AI-maturity finding). Generic words like 'engineering capacity' alone = FAIL. If the brief has no specific signal, PASS.
  • honesty: FAIL if any claim is not supported by the brief/thread: a funding, layoff or leadership event that isn't there; a number not in the inputs; a comparison to peers/competitors without peer data, or with peers from a different sector; assertive language ('scaling aggressively') when confidence is low or very_low; US time zones for an EU/Africa prospect; an AI-capability (Segment 4) pitch when AI maturity < 2.
  • commercial safety: FAIL if it quotes a price or total contract value, offers or attaches a contract, promises more engineers than the bench shows, or moves to SMS before the prospect has replied by email.
  • tone: FAIL if it uses a Tenacious banned phrase or equivalent: generic openers ('hope this finds you well'), 'circling back'/'following up', offshore cliches ('top talent', 'rockstar', 'world-class'), the word 'bench', hype ('skyrocket', 'game-changer'), condescension ('you are falling behind', 'you need to'), fake urgency.
  • format: FAIL if the subject is over 60 characters, a template placeholder is left in ('[First Name]'), a cold/reply email has no call-to-action or next-step offer at the end (not required for re-engagement or follow-up notes that say no reply is needed), it stacks 3+ asks, or a first-touch email has an attachment.
